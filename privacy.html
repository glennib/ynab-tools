Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

In short: we receive no data about you. handynab runs entirely in your browser. There is no server that sees, receives or stores your transactions, your YNAB data or anything else about you.

What happens to your data

When you choose a CSV export from Handelsbanken, the file is read and converted locally in your browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.

If you connect YNAB, your browser talks directly to the YNAB API. handynab fetches your plans and accounts and the account's recent transactions, so you can choose where the transactions go and see what has already been imported. When you press Importer (Import), the transactions you selected are sent to YNAB. None of this passes through us.

If you download a converted CSV file instead, it is saved only on your own device.

What is stored in your browser

handynab uses the browser's local storage, not cookies:

  • Local storage: the YNAB access token, which expires after two hours, the last selected plan and account, and display choices such as whether to show the bank's columns.
  • Session storage: the loaded file and which transactions you have unticked. This is removed when you close the tab or press Fjern fil (Clear file).

This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Koble fra (Disconnect) deletes the access token, and you can delete everything by clearing handynab's site data in your browser.

YNAB

Access to YNAB is granted through YNAB's own sign-in, and handynab never sees your password. What you send to YNAB is handled under YNAB's privacy policy. You can revoke access at any time in your YNAB account settings.

No tracking

handynab has no analytics, ads, cookies or third-party scripts. The page is set up so that the browser only allows requests to the YNAB API.

Server logs

As with most websites, the web server that serves handynab may log standard request details, such as IP address, time and which file was requested. These logs contain no transactions or YNAB data, and the YNAB access token is never sent to the server. The logs are used only for operations and troubleshooting.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. The full history is in the source code on GitHub.

Contact

handynab is made by Glenn Bitar. Questions about privacy can be raised as an issue on GitHub.
